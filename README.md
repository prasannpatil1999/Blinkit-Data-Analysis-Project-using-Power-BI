### Project Overview
The BlinkIT Grocery Data.xlsx file contains essential columns such as product details, customer information, order timestamps, and sales data, providing a comprehensive view of business operations. This structured dataset enables in-depth analysis of purchasing patterns, inventory management, and delivery performance.

### Tools Used
- Power BI
- Power Query

### Data Sources
- BlinkIT Grocery Data.xlsx file contains Grocery sales data related to Blinkit online delivery platform

### Data Cleaning/Preparation
- In the initial data preparation phase, we performed the following tasks:

1. Data importing into power query and inspection
2. Assigned appropriate data types to columns
4. Applied Power Query transformations: removed duplicates, handled null values.
5. Corrected typos, and inconsistencies in Item Fat Content field.
7. Wrote DAX functions to analyze the data.

### Insights & Findings
- Total sales is 1.2 Million Rupees
- Average Sales is 141 Rupees
- No of Items sold is 8.52 Thousand
- Average Rating is 3.9
- Low fat content items have made more sales than regular one
- Fruitsnad vegetables, then snacks foods are top2 item types that made most revenue
- In yeasr 2018 most sales were made
- Tier 3 Outlet location type have made most sales
- Tier 3 Outlet location type Low fat content items have made most sales
- Medium sized outlets have made most sales

### Recommendations
- Promote Low-Fat Products
→ Expand and market low-fat product lines, especially in regions with high demand.

- Boost Fruits, Vegetables & Snacks
→ Prioritize stock and targeted promotions for top-selling categories to maximize revenue.

- Target Tier 3 Locations
→ Invest in logistics, inventory, and marketing in Tier 3 areas where sales are strongest.

- Leverage Medium-Sized Outlets
→ Optimize operations and replicate success models from high-performing medium outlets.

- Average Rating is 3.9
→ Work on improving average ratings through customer feedback and product improvements.

- In year 2018 most sales were made
→ Analyze and replicate successful 2018 strategies in current campaigns.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, the Blinkit Data Analysis Project provided valuable insights into customer preferences, product performance, and regional trends. Low-fat products, fruits, and snacks emerged as top performers, with Tier 3 locations and medium-sized outlets leading in sales. These findings highlight key areas for strategic focus and growth. Leveraging these insights can help optimize inventory, marketing, and customer satisfaction.
